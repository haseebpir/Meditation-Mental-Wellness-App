## ✨ Overview

**MindEase** is a meditation and mental wellness app designed to help users relax, focus, and build healthier daily habits. With guided sessions, mood tracking, and calming soundscapes, it turns a few minutes a day into meaningful mental wellness routine.

---

## 💼 Why Users (and Clients) Love This

- 🧘 **Guided Meditations** — Sessions for stress, focus, sleep, and anxiety, led by calming narration
- 🌙 **Sleep Stories & Soundscapes** — Relaxing audio designed to help users fall asleep faster
- 📊 **Mood & Mindfulness Tracking** — Simple daily check-ins that reveal patterns over time
- 🔥 **Streaks & Gentle Reminders** — Encourages consistency without feeling pushy or stressful
- 🤖 **AI-Personalized Sessions** — Recommendations adapt based on mood, goals, and progress
- 🎧 **Offline Listening** — Sessions and soundscapes available without an internet connection
- 📅 **Daily Wellness Plans** — Structured programs for building a lasting mindfulness habit
- 🧑‍🏫 **Expert-Led Content** — Sessions created with input from mental wellness practitioners
- 📈 **Progress Insights** — Visual summaries showing consistency and emotional trends
- 🔒 **Private & Judgment-Free** — Mood data kept fully private, encouraging honest self check-ins

---

## 🧱 How It Works

| Feature | Experience |
|---|---|
| **Check In** | Users log how they're feeling in a quick, simple daily check-in |
| **Get Guided** | AI-personalized meditation and sleep sessions match their current mood |
| **Build the Habit** | Gentle streaks and reminders encourage daily consistency |
| **Reflect on Progress** | Mood trends and insights show real emotional progress over time |
| **Relax Anytime** | Offline access means calm is always just a tap away |

## 🛠️ Tech Stack (Under the Hood)

**Mobile App:** React Native (single codebase for iOS & Android)
**Backend:** Node.js, TypeScript for content delivery, mood data, and personalization
**AI Layer:** Personalization engine matching sessions to user mood and goals
**Cloud Infrastructure:** AWS for secure, scalable, always-available hosting

This stack keeps the experience **calm, personal, and reliable**, whether streaming a session or reviewing mood history.
